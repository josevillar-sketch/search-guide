## How to use this sheet

- A fact is here only if both independent checks confirmed it, or if I re-read the primary source myself and it held. Corrections from the checks are applied. Facts the checks reported missing are added only after I re-read the source.
- Tags: **[2 checks]** = both checks confirmed and the wording is unchanged. **[corrected]** = confirmed, with a correction applied (the note says what changed). **[added]** = new fact I re-read in the source today.
- "Words" are the College Board's words, copied from the source. Quote them exactly, or paraphrase them in plain words. Do not add to them.
- House voice (BOOK_AUTHORING and house brief): write "percent", not "%". Write times as words, never "p.m. ET". Examples: "noon, local time", "11:59 in the evening, Eastern Time". Use "Part A" and "Part B", "the exam", and "Source A".
- Room 63 choices go in a WAITING ON A ROOM 63 DECISION card, never as fact. Section R lists them.
- CED page numbers are the printed page numbers of the AP Seminar Course and Exam Description (Revised Edition, Effective Fall 2024, © 2024). This CED is still in force for 2026-27.
