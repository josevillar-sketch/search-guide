# Book request list: AP Research methods library

For Room 63, Mater Academy. Prepared 2026-11-11 for Mr. Portela. Every edition and ISBN below was confirmed against a publisher page or at least two bookseller or library listings on 2026-11-11.

Ask for the **paperback** ISBN. When a choice exists, the newer edition is listed.

## Tier 1: request first

These three replace most of what SAGE Research Methods would give students.

| # | Book | Edition, year | Publisher | Paperback ISBN | Why it matters in AP Research |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Creswell, J. W., & Creswell, J. D. *Research Design: Qualitative, Quantitative, and Mixed Methods Approaches* | 6th, 2022 | SAGE | 9781071817940 | The standard text students cite to justify their method. Covers all three designs. |
| 2 | Booth, W. C., Colomb, G. G., Williams, J. M., Bizup, J., & FitzGerald, W. T. *The Craft of Research* | 5th, 2024 | University of Chicago Press | 9780226826677 | Question to argument to presentation. Readable for high school; the new edition adds a chapter on presenting research. |
| 3 | Machi, L. A., & McEvoy, B. T. *The Literature Review: Six Steps to Success* | 5th, 2025 | Corwin | 9781071974896 | Step-by-step literature review. The 5th edition adds guidance on using AI ethically in each step. The 4th edition (2022, ISBN 9781071852903) is an acceptable fallback. |

## Tier 2: by method

Request the ones that match the methods your students choose.

| # | Book | Edition, year | Publisher | Paperback ISBN | For students who |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | Braun, V., & Clarke, V. *Thematic Analysis: A Practical Guide* | 1st, 2021 | SAGE | 9781473953246 | Analyze interviews or open-ended answers. Thematic analysis is the most common qualitative method in AP Research papers. |
| 5 | Saldaña, J. *The Coding Manual for Qualitative Researchers* | 5th, 2025 | SAGE | 9781529680003 | Code interview or text data. Expensive and long; one teacher copy is enough. |
| 6 | Krippendorff, K. *Content Analysis: An Introduction to Its Methodology* | 4th, 2018 | SAGE | 9781506395661 | Analyze media, documents, or social posts. |
| 7 | Fowler, F. J. *Survey Research Methods* | 5th, 2013 | SAGE | 9781452259000 | Design surveys: sampling and question writing. Short. Older, so pair with Dillman for online surveys. |
| 8 | Wheelan, C. *Naked Statistics: Stripping the Dread from the Data* | 2014 | W. W. Norton | 9780393347777 | Report numbers without a statistics course. Plain language. |

## Already free (no request needed)

- Bhattacherjee, A. *Social Science Research: Principles, Methods, and Practices* (2012). Open textbook, free PDF: https://digitalcommons.usf.edu/oa_textbooks/3/

## Suggested quantities

- Tier 1: one copy per AP Research section, plus a teacher copy.
- Tier 2: one shared classroom copy each; Saldaña and Krippendorff as teacher copies only.
- Tier 3: one copy each.
